## About Me

I’m passionate about developing AI systems that are not only intelligent but also impactful and accessible for people. My work spans computer vision, natural language processing, and robotics, where I focus on solving challenging problems and exploring new approaches.

I am pursuing an MSc in Artificial Intelligence and Robotics at [Sapienza University](https://www.uniroma1.it/) and hold a BSc from [Bocconi University](https://www.unibocconi.it/), where my thesis introduced multi-stage anonymized video synthesis via generative motion transfer. I have published at [ICCV 2025 CV4BIOM Workshop](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/ICCV2025_workshops/CV4BIOM/) on expression-consistent talking-head anonymization with the [INRIA STARS](https://team.inria.fr/stars/) team under [Dr. Antitza Dantcheva](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=ZMggPHMAAAAJ). I am currently a research assistant at the [VRAI Lab](https://vrai.dii.univpm.it/) with [Prof. Rocco Pietrini](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=SKuHmqUAAAAJ/), working on cross-view video synchronization and applied vision for personalized color analysis.

I thrive on collaboration with people who are excited about pushing the boundaries of AI, and I find the most rewarding part of this field is turning research into solutions that truly matter.

Always open to connecting with like‑minded innovators and exploring how we can contribute to the future of AI together.
